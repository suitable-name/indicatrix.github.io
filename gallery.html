How & Where to Add Pictures of Specific App Parts

Follow this simple guide whenever you capture new screenshots or renders of the application.

1. Image Directory Location

All website images live in the assets/images/ folder:
Absolute path: /mnt/c/temp/mcp/ghp/assets/images/

2. Recommended Screenshot Topics & Filenames

Here are key parts of the app that make great visual additions, along with their suggested filenames:

Application Feature Suggested Filename Optimal Aspect Ratio
Solid Inspection View (B-Rep mesh, facet picking, critical angle overlay) solid_inspection.png 16:10 or 16:9 (e.g. 1920×1200)
Faceting Tier Editor & Meet Solver (Editing angles, constraints, solver status) editor_solve.png 16:10 or 16:9
Material Retargeting Dialog (Recutting angles for different RI) retarget_dialog.png Modal Dialog (~800×600 or 16:10)
In-Browser WebGPU App (indicatrix-web running in browser tab) web_demo.png 16:9 (e.g. 1920×1080)
High-Res Converged Master Renders (Diamond, Ruby, Alexandrite fire) master_render_diamond.png 1:1 square or 16:9
Rough Preform & Volumetric Yield (Cylinder/cone rough fit) preform_yield.png 16:10

3. How to Link or Embed a New Picture in HTML

Once you save the PNG file into assets/images/, you can display it anywhere on the site by adding a gallery card:

HTML — Add to gallery.html or index.html
<div class="gallery-card"> <div class="gallery-img-wrapper"> <img src="assets/images/solid_inspection.png" alt="Solid Inspection View" class="gallery-img"> </div> <div class="gallery-info"> <span class="gallery-tag">CAD Inspection</span> <h3 class="gallery-title">B-Rep Polyhedral Mesh Inspection</h3> <p class="gallery-text"> Interactive facet picking with critical-angle risk warning overlays. </p> </div> </div>

4. Updating Existing Screenshots

To replace any of the current screenshots with an updated capture from a newer build, simply overwrite the existing files (main.png, settings.png, tilt_performance.png) in assets/images/. The website will automatically display the updated images without needing any HTML changes!
